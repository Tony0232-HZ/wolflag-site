WOLFLAG is the China's leading manufacturer and supplier of flags, banners, bunting, flagpoles, exhibition stands, temporary signage and promotional displays. We are also Official Flag Suppliers to the China Government. Using our extensive experience, a wide range of materials including PVC, knitted polyester, canvas, vinyl and paper, and the latest in printing technology, we help businesses to get noticed with creative, eye catching and high impact promotional and advertising solutions.

Whether you're organising a product launch or promotional campaign, running an event or attending an exhibition or ensuring your corporate image is displayed to maximum effect, WOLFLAG's complete, customer focused service and varied, yet concentrated, product range, will provide you with a professional display solution suited to your environment, brand image and budget.

Our foreign trade sales headquarters is located in Hangzhou (Hangzhou Loyal Import & Export Co., Ltd) and boasts an excellent sales team, offering highly experienced services to global flag purchasers. With other two offices in Jiaxing and Yuyao we are always on hand to discuss your requirements and advise on the right solution for you. From design and print at our Jiaxing site, to deliver to your site, your flag, flag pole, banner, building wrap or promotional display order will be project managed from start to finish.

We, as a factory, ship our products directly to you with very low shipping cost. There are no distributors or "middle-men" involved, so you get the lowest price each and every time you buy. This will save you money compared to your local shop.

A wide range of national flags, table flags, flag poles, bunting and hand wavers are available from our website. If for any reason you are unable to find exactly what you are looking for, call one of our knowledgeable customer service representatives who will be happy to assist.
